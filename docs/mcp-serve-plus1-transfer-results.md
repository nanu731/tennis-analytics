unsmoothed personal transfer design earns no factor advancement. Men's primary
losses favor the reference; women's gains conflict with attempt-weighted losses
and one player deletion. Endpoint failure remains explicit, not a reason to
search smoothing parameters. Ownership, parser correctness and larger counts
are not factor validity. This result concerns only observed +1 attempts selected
by earlier Serve/Return actions; it neither measures all Rally play nor rejects
Rally or Return as research domains.

opponent/earlier-action selection and unknown opportunities limit interpretation.
