linked match/point/action identities and event times spanning all three stages.

not validated data. Download controls below mean **advertised access**, not a
successful binary download; authentication, byte integrity and contents remain

## Opportunity assessment and attribution

**Conditional assessment proposal, not an implemented measurement:** synchronized,
calibrated observed ball trajectories and both-player locations could describe
the server's position relative to the arriving returned ball immediately before
+1. Serve and Return contact times, bounces, player identities and point/action
linkage would connect the delivery and response to that opportunity. Position
and arrival information could motivate a later test of a *predictive* opportunity
measure. No source above validates such a measure for this sample.

An event's final trajectory, contact location observed only at its end, eventual
winner, post-action landing label, or reconstruction using later frames cannot
be silently installed in a pre-action predictor. Retrospective estimates require
explicit availability/observation flags and a declared boundary; a nominal
frame timestamp alone is insufficient. A derived attack/defence or shot-quality
label also needs its information basis checked.

Even complete tracking would not isolate the serve's causal contribution from
receiver ability/choice, serving side, spin, unmeasured context and selective
return survival. Serve owns delivery, Return owns response and +1 remains
Rally-owned. Disjoint ledger ownership is not statistical independence or
permission to transfer Return credit. Predictive opportunity measurement,
contemporaneous net-point association, stable transfer and factor qualification
remain separate scientific requirements. No causal, NPR, factor or forecast
claim follows from this search.

provider/author documentation. Several original or auxiliary routes could not

actual file schema, usable-match count or permission beyond the stated licenses
was verified. No request/application/outreach was submitted.

Focused repository checks cover four-file scope, current-document agreement,
local links/anchors, whitespace and SHA-256 preservation of 655 historical
files plus three historical document tails. No analysis or historical test suite
is rerun for this documentation-only change. The final Git record is reported
