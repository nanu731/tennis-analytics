The expanded support audit's all-context +1 inventory is a reconciliation total,

Serve +1 is the server's first physical post-return STROKE, including an attempted
error. It is owned exclusively by Rally: ordinal three identifies the attempted
stroke, including errors, rather than treating rallyCount as an attempt count.
Volleys, overheads and serve-and-volley +1 strokes retain their frozen ownership.
Administrative challenges create no stroke. +1 receives no extra factor-total
credit; FIRST/SECOND are contexts within one Rally measurement, not two factors.

For neutral server p, match m and context c:

- N[p,m,c]: frozen-QA-usable +1 physical attempts with supported pre-action
  FIRST/SECOND context and an observed binary error/nonerror annotation.
- E[p,m,c]: those N attempts annotated FORCED_ERROR or UNFORCED_ERROR, preserving
  both subcounts. WINNER or a supported nonterminal in-play stroke is a known
  nonerror; this does not mean successful play or superior shot quality.
- The conditional annotated-error frequency is E/N only for N>0; otherwise
  undefined. Pool component counts before division, never match percentages.

UNKNOWN_ERROR_TYPE means an unknown subtype/location. A documented #/@ terminal
still establishes the binary error annotation and remains in N/E, with the
unknown subtype explicit. An unknown binary label never becomes a success or
error and receives no labeled denominator credit. Optional unknown shot type or
direction does not erase a supported attempt or known binary annotation.
Unsupported notation or a decoded prefix cannot promote full-point QA.

Attempt contexts use only the saved pre-action boundary, pending Rally/server,
two prior in-play strokes and recorded pre_serve_number, with provenance to the
prior serve/state action. FIRST=1, SECOND=2; missing number is UNKNOWN and an
unsupported prior boundary/phase is UNSUPPORTED. Current error, winner, shot
attributes, raw current token or later action cannot determine these contexts.
Full-point and completion QA are retrospective availability gates, not predictors.

Preserve separate point dispositions: VALIDATED_ATTEMPT, KNOWN_ABSENT,
UNVALIDATED_OBSERVED_PREFIX and UNKNOWN_OPPORTUNITY. Also distinguish validated
attempts withheld by score QA, unknown binary labels and unsupported contexts.
Known absence has NOT_APPLICABLE execution context, no attempt and no execution
success; never put it in N or encode it as a zero error trial. Unknown opportunity
without an observed prefix has UNKNOWN context. Unvalidated prefixes retain their
observed action and pre-context, but enter neither E nor N. Their unobserved
suffix multiplicity is NOT_IDENTIFIABLE. Keep raw notation and source IDs.

The estimand is future **annotated error frequency conditional on reaching a
charted, QA-usable +1 attempt with observed context and binary annotation**, in
this frozen sample. Earlier serve and return actions select these opportunities;
it is not an error rate per service point, overall Rally effectiveness, causal
responsibility, isolated player skill, net-point association or independent factor.
Exclusive action ownership does not establish statistical independence.

## Proposed ALL_DATE_EXPANDING experiment

For every same-category/source-date batch, freeze:

q_personal[p,c,t] = sum(E_prior_player_context) / sum(N_prior_player_context)

q_reference[c,t] = sum(E_prior_all_servers_context) / sum(N_prior_all_servers_context)

Use only eligible same-category records with source_date strictly earlier than
the target batch, across all surfaces. Use all earlier batches; no history window,
smoothing, clipping, imputation, adjustment or adequacy threshold. The reference
is a comparator and cannot initialize a missing personal history. Equal-date
matches are simultaneous: none updates another's estimate. Every match and its
points stay together; source-date order is assumed, not verified availability.

The same internal completion gate applies to targets, personal histories and
references: known status cues, incomplete charts, unresolved terminal conflicts
and missing completion evidence fail closed. Official completion remains
NOT_VERIFIED; passing internal replay is sufficient only for this conditional
experiment. All sampled/excluded rows remain in coverage.

Freeze one identical personal/reference mask by category/match/neutral slot/context,
using completion, known context, nonseed target and positive target/personal/reference
N. Precedence: completion exclusion; unknown/unsupported context; earliest eligible
batch seed; zero usable target N; zero personal prior N; zero reference N; paired
support. Preserve every underlying reason/count even when precedence controls.
Errors, error frequencies and comparative losses cannot select or replace rows.
Positive sparse N permits an estimate, not a claim of adequacy.

Assess FIRST and SECOND separately within each historical category. Primary cell
Brier is [E*(1-q)^2+(N-E)*q^2]/N. Average supported server-slot losses within each
whole match, then matches equally. Secondary natural-log loss uses
-[E*log(q)+(N-E)*log(1-q)]/N with explicit 0*log(0)=0; contradictory unsmoothed
q=0/q=1 yields Inf, retained with its cause. Secondary opportunity-weighted losses
pool sums over attempt counts. Personal-minus-reference is negative when personal
loss is lower descriptively. Inf-minus-Inf is indeterminate; missing comparisons
are UNEVALUABLE, never zero loss. No context pooling or composite factor score.

Enumerate every eligible nonseed evaluation match and both-slot player identity,
including zero-impact deletions. Delete whole evaluation matches containing that
player in either slot, retain frozen predictions and all training histories, and
rescore without refitting. Empty remainders abstain. Report E/N, observed/usable/
withheld exposure, all dispositions, sparse denominators, history depths and
source-date gaps alongside results. Require batch and recurring-player dependence;
retain UNCERTAINTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED with no interval, p-value or superiority claim.

Stop on changed pins, incomplete inventory, duplicate/ambiguous identity, invalid
date, parser/state/point authority conflict, invalid counts, E>N, inconsistent
subcounts, cutoff violation or mismatch with frozen support. No repair or rescue.
Endpoint numerical behavior cannot establish transferable information.

All acquired years including 2026 are exposed. This overlapping retrospective
experiment would not be independent replication or an untouched final test.
Do not pool it with prior Serve/Return releases as separate evidence. No NPR
correlation, weight, rating, action value or match-winning forecast is authorized.
Execution requires the exact approval at the end after measured support review.
