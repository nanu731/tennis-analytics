## Factor construction and fitting safeguards

For each neutral player slot, pool eligible earlier counts before division; never average match-level rates:

| Metric | Pooled numerator / denominator |
| --- | --- |
| M03 | Own first-serve points won / own first serves in |
| M05 | Own double faults / (own service points minus own first serves in) |
| M11 | Opponent break points faced / opponent service games |
| M12 | Opponent (break points faced minus saved) / opponent break points faced |

Rates exist only for positive pooled denominators. Empty histories and zero-opportunity rates stay undefined with reasons. Require all four rates in both slots and finite A-minus-B differences for the shared factor training/target cohort. Removing M05 does not expand eligibility. M05 keeps its lower-is-better orientation; differences are not sign-flipped.

Full S08 uses dM03/dM05/dM11/dM12; reduced uses dM03/dM11/dM12. For each tour/fold, use base-R binomial logistic regression with intercept fixed at zero: `eta = sum(beta_j * dM_j / sd_train_j)` and `p(A wins) = plogis(eta)`. Use training-fold sample SD only, without mean centering. Retained predictors have the same training SDs across the two fits because IDs are identical; coefficients are separately estimated. Target-slot swaps negate eta and complement p. No intercept alternative, interaction, surface/event indicator, nonlinear term, Elo feature, sign constraint or imputation is allowed.

Preserve [Phase 2R safeguards](s08-paired-evaluation-results.md#prospective-correction-and-implementation) and the [Phase 2W dimension change](m05-ablation-results.md#prospective-comparison-registration). The saved numerical definitions in [Phase 2R code](../R/run_s08_paired_evaluation.R) and [Phase 2W code](../R/run_m05_ablation.R) are authoritative for implementation, not permission to execute their historical runners:

## Frozen Elo arithmetic

Reuse the [Phase 2P implementation](surface-elo-baseline-audit.md#implementation-and-authority-boundary): initial G and every S are 1500; scale 400; fixed K=32. Primary rating `R=0.5G+0.5S` on the target surface; overall-only sensitivity uses G. `P(A)=1/(1+10^((R_B−R_A)/400))`. Blend ratings, not probabilities.

All match expectations use frozen pre-batch states. Overall A delta is `32*(y−p_G)`; target-surface A delta is `32*(y−p_S)` using independent component expectations. Opponent deltas are negatives. Sum each player's deltas across the whole simultaneous batch, then apply them; sum canonically for reproducible floating-point serialization, not as chronology. No averaging, clipping or sequential within-batch update. Untouched surfaces remain unchanged; unseen surfaces start at 1500 rather than copying G.

Every admitted match, including factor-incomplete rows and rows without factor predictions, updates later Elo states. Excluded records never update any state. No round, prestige, margin, format, inactivity, decay or experience-dependent K adjustment; no tuning. Cold-start flags and model-specific availability remain reported. Elo priors do not impute S08 histories.

Assess both model gates in every such batch, including those with no complete targets. Do not define required folds retrospectively as only successful or scored folds. An unattemptable or failed fold remains a failure for selection even if later folds succeed. Structural target-history incompleteness is a reported coverage loss, not itself a failed model gate; it cannot expand the shared cohort or be hidden. Missing/blocked inputs, an empty tour comparison, nonfinite losses or an unevaluable whole-batch deletion cannot establish a preference. Absent panel cells remain reported scope limitations; no new official-coverage gate or claimed all-panel validation is invented.

For each tour t and primary metric m let `D(t,m)` be reduced-minus-full mean loss on the common mask. A strict positive value favors full and a strict negative value favors reduced. Use unrounded stored values; no tolerance or practical-equivalence margin. A full-sample exact tie does not satisfy “lower.” For a candidate that is strictly better in both metrics and tours, require no direction reversal in either metric after **any** whole-batch score deletion in either tour. An exact zero after deletion is a tie, not a reversal under the user's rule; disclose it without claiming preserved strict advantage. Empty/undefined retained comparisons cannot satisfy this safeguard.
