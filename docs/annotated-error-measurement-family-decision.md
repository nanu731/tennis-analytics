## Endpoint behavior, weighting and the factor gap

[Broader Return endpoint evidence](mcp-broader-return-transfer-results.md#infinite-losses--preserved-not-rescued)
records three women SECOND zero estimates contradicted by target errors, yielding
Inf log loss. The fixed pseudocount makes all 312 estimates interior and removes
those infinities, yet three groups still favor the reference and the remaining
whole-match gain changes direction under weighting/deletions. Finite numbers
therefore demonstrate numerical stabilization, not useful player information.

Both first-fault releases have finite losses and no observed endpoint failures;
this still supplies no cross-category factor qualification. Serve +1 has 27
contradictions: 21 q=1 followed by nonerrors and six q=0 followed by errors.
All four personal log losses remain Inf while reference losses are finite.
Brier can remain finite despite these failures. No endpoint rescue, new smoothing
or outcome-selected exclusion is adopted.

Whole-match loss weights matches equally after averaging supported player slots;
opportunity-weighted loss weights the same IDs by observed attempt counts. They
answer different questions. Women's pseudocount SECOND and both +1 contexts
change direction between weightings. Keep the registered primary assessment
and disclose the secondary conflict; do not pick whichever looks favorable.
Deletion diagnostics describe composition influence, not uncertainty intervals.

These frequencies record narrow execution events. First faults omit the benefit
of a successful serve, second-serve outcomes and opponent response. Return
errors omit what an in-play return achieves; +1 errors omit its pressure,
placement and later point consequence. Forced/unforced annotations do not measure
causal responsibility or latent shot difficulty. A complement called "in-play
rate" is not a new mechanism: the [execution dictionary](mcp-execution-summary-audit.md#candidate-metric-recommendation-and-limitations)
identifies the in-play/error partition and winners as an in-play subset.

The intended factors should represent distinct, interpretable sources of point
advantage, with manageable collinearity, stable incremental explanatory
information and future value. Exclusive ownership prevents duplicate ledger
assignment; it does not establish low correlation or conditional independence.
Reliable transfer of an annotation would still not prove explanatory value for
Net Point Rating. These action-error experiments test neither contemporaneous
NPR association, cross-domain collinearity, unique NPR increments nor joint
weights. Historical aggregate-statistic NPR findings cannot be transferred to
new action metrics. No such relationship is claimed to have been checked here.

## One proposed next question: direct Serve terminal balance

**PROPOSED_DIRECT_SERVE_TERMINAL_OUTCOME_TRANSFER; NOT_IMPLEMENTED.**
Can a player's prior distribution of direct Serve terminal outcomes transfer to
later whole matches beyond a same-category reference, providing a point-benefit/
point-cost measurement rather than another isolated error frequency?

Use existing pinned MCP_EXPANDED_RECURRING_1.0.0 action/state and point-
reconciliation ledgers. The [saved execution dictionary](mcp-execution-summary-audit.md#prospective-count-and-denominator-dictionary)
already supports ACE and DOUBLE_FAULT and a fully validated service-point
denominator; [state authority](mcp-action-state-audit.md#prospective-schema-and-information-boundary)
separates terminal outcomes from pre-action information. No tracking, movement,
shot-quality inference, new source or expanded sample is needed for this narrow
observable question. Actual expanded support/retention for this new denominator
has not been measured in this decision; do not copy the first-fault or +1 masks.

Proposed measurement: `(ACE_points - DOUBLE_FAULT_points) / N_service_points`.
N is unique fully validated, score-reconciled service points for that neutral
server in completion-eligible charts, with supported terminal authority. This
is a point denominator, deliberately different from physical first attempts.
Every retained point has one class: ACE, DOUBLE_FAULT or OTHER. ACE/DOUBLE_FAULT
require a usable nonvoid Serve-owned terminal action agreeing with point/server
reconciliation; mutually conflicting terminals fail closed. OTHER means a fully
validated point without either direct terminal, not execution success. All other
points stay explicitly unknown/withheld; zero N remains undefined. Preserve all
223 inventory charts, completion exclusions and raw identifiers.

Use only literal ACE and DOUBLE_FAULT meanings. Do not expand ACE to collapsed
UNRETURNABLE notation, distinct Return errors, challenges or all service points
won. UNRETURNABLE and points ending in Return/Rally or administrative actions
are OTHER only when independently validated, with their distinctions retained
in coverage. This tests a deliberately partial Serve component, not total serve
quality or causal Serve Action Value. The +/-1 is point bookkeeping, not an
estimated importance weight.

Plausible mechanism: direct Serve endings earn a point without a subsequent
Return/Rally stroke, or concede one through double fault. This includes a benefit
as well as a cost, addressing the named construct gap in fault-only frequency.
It does not claim that a first fault causes a loss or that aces measure isolated
skill. Ace incidence can reflect receiver quality and context.

Potential overlap: the balance contains points also present in NPR's numerator;
a same-match association would be partly algebraic and cannot validate it.
Double faults overlap the second-serve-error family; aces are a subset of broader
unreturned-service outcomes. Its opportunity distribution changes Return/Rally
exposure even though its terminals are exclusively Serve-owned. Do not add the
same point credit to another domain or infer low collinearity from exclusivity.
Later explanatory/incremental and joint-dependence work would remain separate.

Falsifiable first assessment, proposed for separate approval:

- Preserve the frozen sample/completion/QA gates and historical category labels.
  Reconstruct unique point classes, denominator and support independently before
  scoring; stop on authority or accounting conflicts, without repairs.
- Use ALL_DATE_EXPANDING simultaneous same-category/source-date batches.
  Personal and category-reference three-class probabilities pool only strictly
  earlier eligible service-point counts; no current-batch information enters.
  Prior and target positive N are required; cold starts abstain on one identical
  mask. Apply no smoothing, tuning, window, new restriction or adequacy threshold.
- Primary multiclass Brier is the sum of squared differences between the three
  predicted probabilities and the point's 0/1 class indicators. Average points
  within server-match slot, supported slots within match, then matches equally.
  Keep categories separate. Secondary natural-log and opportunity-weighted
  losses retain zero-probability contradictions as Inf. Report expected balance
  `p_ACE - p_DOUBLE_FAULT` and both component counts so cancellation is visible.
- Enumerate frozen-prediction whole-match and both-slot player deletions without
  refitting. Report all-inventory dispositions, retention, prior exposure and
  loss directions beside each other. No new threshold or composite selection
  score: reference-favored, mixed, nonfinite or composition-sensitive evidence
  cannot be called a qualified factor. Favorable descriptive transfer would
  support only further research into this component, not final qualification.

This is one new observable point-outcome hypothesis, not another smoothing
search or a generic readiness audit. No comparative loss, probability, balance,
support count or NPR statistic for it is calculated now. Missing authority or no
positive-denominator paired targets must be disclosed and require user direction;
do not automatically expand or substitute another metric.
