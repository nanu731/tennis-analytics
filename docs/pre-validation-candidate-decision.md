## Why both advance, and what remains blocked

Occam's rule favors the smaller model when complexity earns no stable value; it does not convert an unestablished, near-zero difference into proof of equality. The reduced comparator is necessary to test whether M05 earns its place. Full-only advancement would drop that direct question despite the direction conflict. Reduced-only advancement would resolve the four-component question from dependent development evidence that does not establish M05's stable lack of value. Advancing neither would stop a bounded, executable scientific comparison despite intact measurement and numerical safeguards. Advancing both therefore preserves two distinct prespecified tests, without claiming that the full model has passed the earlier interpretation gate or that validation can automatically erase its failed direction compatibility.

A later result favoring full S08 numerically cannot by itself cure the WTA construct conflict. A result favoring reduced cannot turn three components into Four Factors. Final selection may remain inconclusive or support neither; final factor qualification remains separate from forecast model comparison. S02 is not reinstated. No replacement, additional candidate, threshold, weights or search is introduced.
