# Phase 2U: M05 performance and forecast interpretation review

## Decision

**M05_REVISION_REQUIRED_BEFORE_VALIDATION.** The interpretation needs clarification before progression to 2024 validation; this is a review decision and revision recommendation, not an implemented factor change or automatic pause of S08. The present definition, lower-is-better orientation, factor/model status, coefficients and predictions remain unchanged. S02 stays paused and S08 provisional.

M05 directly measures the observed double-fault rate per second-serve opportunity. It captures one error component of second-serve security, not the full ability to win points after missing a first serve. Its negative contemporaneous performance relationships support that narrower descriptive role. Its historical forecast coefficients do not establish a common, directionally consistent security mechanism across tours. Within-tour repetition of a coefficient sign is not the same as replication of the prespecified lower-is-better interpretation.

**One bounded revision question:** should performance reporting explicitly label the unchanged M05 formula “double-fault rate per second-serve opportunity,” while separating that lower-is-better descriptive interpretation from its unqualified, tour-specific historical forecast association? This role clarification is recommended for separate approval. It neither selects another second-serve candidate nor resolves whether M05 earns a place in a future forecast model.

## Role-specific conclusions and consequences

**Performance factor:** the numerator and denominator have a direct, auditable meaning. A lower observed error frequency can be described as fewer double faults per second-serve opportunity. It cannot, by definition alone, rank total second-serve effectiveness: points after successful delivery and other aspects of the candidate family are outside the numerator. Prefer the precise double-fault-rate label in the proposed revision; “second-serve risk” could still imply broader tactical or outcome risk not measured here. The current label has not been changed in this phase. Contemporary signs and increments support continued consideration of this narrower component, not final factor approval or a weight.

**Forecast input:** the ATP sign is compatible with the expected direction, while WTA's persistent positive sign conflicts with it. This is an interpretation problem under the registered cross-tour requirement, not proof that the numerical variable can never predict. Conversely, an unconstrained coefficient's freedom to be positive does not satisfy a performance mechanism's direction requirement. A positive WTA coefficient is neither evidence that double faults improve performance nor advice to double-fault more. Aggressiveness, style, suppression, selection, opportunity mix and source measurement remain hypotheses. No coefficient is relabeled as a beneficial performance effect, and no sign constraint is proposed to make the estimates look consistent.

The revision recommendation follows the existing precedence of interpretation and stability over apparent fit; it is not a scored checklist or a new rejection threshold. Relabeling would correct a scope claim but would not cure the WTA direction conflict or establish incremental forecast value. Separating the two roles must not waive the existing final factor-selection gates. Whether a tour-specific association deserves future predictive use remains unresolved and would require its own approved comparison under the standing rules, not an automatic model exemption.

S08's definition and provisional status, S02's pause, memberships, exclusions, histories and predictions remain fixed. This review adds no M05 pause/removal, replacement family, three-factor fit or tour-specific model revision. No historical recommendation or completed report is rewritten. **UNCERTAINTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED**, **SOURCE_LABEL_EVENT_BATCHING_FOR_DEVELOPMENT_SENSITIVITY** and **NO_FORECAST_CHRONOLOGY_SUPPORTED_BY_SAVED_EVIDENCE** persist. The 2024 validation boundary and locked 2025 final test remain untouched. No threshold, fit, tuning, new statistic, dependency, acquisition, OTD, portfolio work or publication occurs.
